# Skill System

All major skills level from 1 to 99.

**Design rule:** Combat classes define how you fight. Skills define how you progress, craft, access the world, and fuel endgame gear.

---

## Woodcutting

**Purpose:** Harvest logs, rare bark, living wood, enchanted timber

**Used for:** Bows, shield cores, trap frames, tool handles, machine braces, ritual wood

**Tree progression:**
1. Ash
2. Oak
3. Willow
4. Ironbark
5. Ghostwood
6. Storm Yew
7. Elderheart
8. Worldroot

**Unlocks:** Faster chopping, rare yield chance, special forestry events, magical grove access

---

## Mining

**Purpose:** Ore, gems, crystal, metals for weapons and armor

**Ore progression:**
1. Copper
2. Tin
3. Iron
4. Coal
5. Silversteel
6. Deep Iron
7. Rune Ore
8. Star Metal
9. Void Shard Veins

**Used for:** Armor, weapons, tools, construct parts, keys, late-game catalysts

**Unlocks:** Stronger pickaxes, rare vein detection, cavern access, high-tier crafting material extraction

---

## Smithing

**Purpose:** Smelt bars, forge weapons, forge armor, improve tools, build forged machine parts

**Progression:**
- Basic bars
- Steel alloys
- Tempered metals
- Rune forging
- Relic forging
- Mythic metalwork

**Unlocks:** Gear crafting, reinforcement slots, set-piece assembly, endgame forging stations

---

## Fishing

**Purpose:** Food, alchemy reagents, bait, rare sea components

**Fish progression:**
1. Riverfish
2. Silverfin
3. Ember Eel
4. Moon Carp
5. Abyss Pike
6. Storm Ray
7. Leviathan Fry

**Used for:** Healing, buff food, rare recipes, boss bait events

**Unlocks:** New rods, dangerous waters, rare catch windows, hidden fishing pools

---

## Cooking

**Purpose:** Convert food to healing and buffs

**Progression:**
- Roast fish
- Field meals
- Fortified stews
- Combat feasts
- Relic banquets

**Effects:** Healing, regen, temporary stat boosts, resistance buffs, raid prep meals

Cooking becomes important in hard PvM because boss food matters, team feast buffs matter, and preparation matters.

---

## Agility

**Purpose:** Movement mastery, traversal, stamina efficiency, dodge timing

**Benefits:**
- Access shortcuts
- Vault obstacles
- Escape mechanics
- Improve sprint economy
- Increase dodge window slightly
- Unlock hidden routes and treasure paths

This skill matters for both exploration and combat.

---

## Strength

**Purpose:** Melee damage scaling, heavy weapon handling, carry capacity, certain object interactions

**Benefits:**
- Higher melee hit value
- Lift barriers
- Wield heavier gear
- Break enemy posture more effectively

---

## Attack

**Purpose:** Melee accuracy, weapon mastery, unlock advanced combat techniques

**Benefits:**
- New weapon categories
- Better hit chance
- Combo chain access
- Stance mastery

Attack and Strength are separate because one governs precision and one governs raw force. That makes builds more interesting.

---

## Prayer

**Purpose:** Buffs, blessing, anti-curse, sacred attunement, support utility

**Examples:**
- Protection aura
- Healing pulse
- Damage blessing
- Corruption cleanse
- Revive window extension
- Relic attunement

Prayer is also used in endgame crafting: sanctifying weapons, awakening relics, blessing armor cores.

---

## Thieving

**Purpose:** Stealth loot, locked chests, pickpocketing, trap disarm, infiltration routes

**Benefits:**
- Rare key fragments
- Smuggled materials
- Black-market recipes
- Hidden boss room access
- Stealth encounter openings

---

## Skill Breakpoint Structure

Use level breakpoints that feel meaningful:

| Range | Phase |
|-------|-------|
| 1–10 | Foundation |
| 11–20 | Identity starts |
| 21–40 | Real specialization begins |
| 41–60 | Midgame mastery |
| 61–80 | Elite preparation |
| 81–99 | Prestige and endgame utility |

**Woodcutting:**
- 1: Ash
- 15: Oak

- 45: Ironbark
- 60: Ghostwood
