# Art Direction

## Visual Style

Premium stylized dark fantasy. Not cartoon cheap. Not muddy realism. Readable, dramatic, expensive.

Think: strong forms, textured materials, rich lighting, high-end VFX, cinematic mood.

---

## Camera

- Elevated 3/4 camera
- Smooth zoom
- Slight rotation in hub and exploration
- Wider framing in boss fights
- Tighter framing in crafting and loot scenes

This gives tactical clarity, gear visibility, co-op readability.

---

## Material Language

Materials need identity.

**Wood:**
- Rough ash
- Polished oak
- Spectral ghostwood
- Storm-lit yew
- Ancient worldroot fibers

**Metal:**
- Iron gray
- Brushed steel
- Silversteel blue
- Rune-metal glow seams
- Celestial alloy with internal shimmer

**Cloth:**
- Stitched rough wool
- Hunter leather
- Blessed wrappings
- Noble battle-cloaks
- Void silk

**Monster parts:**
- Fang ivory
- Obsidian shell
- Ember hide
- Storm membrane
- Luminous bone

If material language is weak, the whole loot game feels fake.

## UI Direction

The UI must feel premium fantasy-tech. Not flat web panels.

**Visual language:**
- Brushed metal
- Carved stone
- Faint rune glow
- Smoked glass overlays
- Rich iconography
- High contrast and clean readability

**Key screens:**
- Inventory
- Equipment comparison
- Skill page
- Talent tree
- Party HUD
- Boss warning UI
- Loot reveal panel
- Map and objective tracker

---

## HUD

Must show:
- Health
- Resource
- Class state
- Cooldowns
- Party status
- Target info
- Consumables
- Key buffs/debuffs

It should feel sharp and expensive, not crowded.

---

## Combat VFX Standards

### Melee
- Clean slash arcs
- Sparks
- Impact shake
- Hit stop
- Armor crack flashes

### Ranged
- Visible projectile trails
- Impact puncture effects
- Weak-point flashes
- Ricochet or split-shot readability

### Magic
- Elegant cast-up
- Sigils
- Trails
- Layered impacts
- Distinct element language

### Prayer
- Sacred rings
- Falling motes
- Gold-white-blue palette
- Calm but powerful visuals

### Engineering/Constructs
- Rotating plates
- Charged cores
- Arc sparks
- Deploy animations
- Repair pulses

---

## Rare Item Presentation

Rare loot needs theater. When a rare item drops:

- The beam is distinct
- The sound is distinct
- The UI reveal is distinct
- The player knows instantly this matters

That moment is part of why people keep playing.

---

## Armor Visual Escalation

| Tier | Look |
|------|------|
| Frontier | Practical, rough, stitched, simple silhouettes |
| Hardened | Reinforced, faction identity begins, more ornament |
| Runic | Glowing seams, stronger silhouette, visible status |
| Mythic | Dramatic shapes, creature-part integration, clear class fantasy |
| Ascendant | Living or legendary quality, aura, motion, unmistakable prestige |

---

## Monster Visual Rules

Each monster should have:

- Strong silhouette
- Readable attack prep
- Environmental fit
- Memorable weak point
- Variant identity across regions

Do not make generic mobs with swapped colors. That cheapens the whole world.
