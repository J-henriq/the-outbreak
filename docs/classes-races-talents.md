# Classes, Races & Talent System

## Races

Races should matter, but not lock players out of content.

### Human

- **Passive: Adaptive Growth** — Gain 3% more all-skill XP until level 50 in each skill
- **Passive: Steady Hands** — Slight boost to weapon swap speed and tool efficiency
- **Best for:** new players, hybrid builds

### Sylvan

- **Passive: Wild Affinity** — Bonus woodcutting, fishing, agility XP
- **Passive: Whisperstep** — Slightly lower enemy detection radius while moving through natural environments
- **Best for:** mobility, ranged, resource play

### Stoneborn

- **Passive: Earthblood** — Bonus mining, smithing, strength XP
- **Passive: Iron Frame** — Higher stagger resistance and physical defense
- **Best for:** tank, melee, crafting-heavy play

### Ashen

- **Passive: Cinder Soul** — Bonus fire and curse resistance
- **Passive: Ember Memory** — Prayer effects and elemental procs last slightly longer
- **Best for:** magic, prayer, hybrid bruisers

### Umbral

- **Passive: Veilwalker** — Bonus thieving and crit chance against unaware enemies
- **Passive: Night Instinct** — Improved weak-point damage and trap evasion
- **Best for:** assassin, stealth, precision

## Classes

Each class has a base kit plus 3 talent paths.

### 1. Vanguard

Frontline bruiser/tank. Uses swords, axes, hammers, shields, heavy armor.

**Core role:** hold aggro, survive mechanics, create openings, protect allies

**Base kit:**
- Guard Stance
- Shield Slam
- Cleaving Strike
- Rally Cry
- Intercept

**Talent path: Bastion**
More defense, taunts, barriers, team damage reduction, retaliation effects

**Talent path: Reaver**
Two-handed aggression, bleed, armor break, execution damage, self-sustain through combat

**Talent path: Warlord**
Support melee, banners, ally buffs, threat manipulation, battlefield control

---

### 2. Ranger

Precision damage dealer. Uses bows, shortbows, traps, mobility tools, light armor.

**Core role:** ranged DPS, target priority, trap placement, weak-point exploitation

**Base kit:**
- Quickshot
- Piercing Arrow
- Rollstep
- Mark Prey
- Snare Trap

**Talent path: Deadeye**
Long-range burst, crit scaling, boss weak-point specialization, charged shot mechanics

**Talent path: Warden Hunt**
Traps, poison, slowing fields, sustained PvM control

**Talent path: Beastbinder**
Summon companion, combo attacks with pet, tracking bonuses, resource-hunting efficiency

---

### 3. Arcanist

Magic damage and control. Uses staffs, foci, ritual seals, robes, medium runic gear.

**Core role:** elemental damage, battlefield denial, debuffs, ritual support

**Base kit:**
- Arc Bolt
- Flame Sigil
- Frost Bind
- Blink
- Arc Ward

**Talent path: Ember Rite**
Fire burst, burning zones, explosive combos, glass cannon

**Talent path: Frostweaver**
Slows, roots, defense shred, team safety tools

**Talent path: Voidcaller**
Curse magic, life drain, anomaly summons, chaos procs

---

### 4. Forgemaster

Combat engineer and crafted-power specialist. Uses gauntlets, hammers, mechanical catalysts, medium armor.

**Core role:** deployables, utility, gear amplification, battlefield setup

**Base kit:**
- Auto-Turret
- Shock Mine
- Reinforce Armor
- Grapple Line
- Repair Pulse

**Talent path: Siegewright**
Stronger constructs, turret specialization, zone denial, boss pressure

**Talent path: Alloy Saint**
Armor support, shield overcharge, defensive tech, repair mastery

**Talent path: Relic Smith**
Relic-powered gadgets, weird arcane effects, rare crafted bonuses, unstable but powerful utility

---

### 5. Nightblade

High-skill mobility assassin. Uses daggers, curved blades, hand-crossbows, stealth tools.

**Core role:** burst, mobility, debuff, pick priority targets, risky high-reward play

**Base kit:**
- Shadowstep
- Hemorrhage
- Smoke Veil
- Sever Tendon
- Phantom Lunge

**Talent path: Executioner**
Huge single-target burst, boss phase punish, weak-point deletion

**Talent path: Veil Dancer**
Stealth loops, evasion, mobility chaining, reposition mastery

**Talent path: Venom Rite**
Poison stacks, debuff spread, sustain damage, anti-healing

## Character Customization

Players create:

- Race
- Body type
- Face
- Scars
- Tattoos
- Hair
- Beard
- Eye glow
- Skin tone
- Voice
- Idle stance
- Homeland accent
- Faction mark
- Class silhouette

Cosmetic identity matters because the player should feel ownership immediately.

---

## Roles in 3-Player Co-op

Strong party examples:

- Vanguard + Ranger + Arcanist
- Forgemaster + Vanguard + Nightblade
- Ranger + Arcanist + Nightblade

The goal is not strict MMO holy trinity locking. The goal is role synergy: pressure, control, sustain, burst, utility.
