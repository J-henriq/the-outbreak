# Gear & Reward Ladder

## Tier 1: Frontier

**Purpose:** Early survival, build introduction, basic identity

**Materials:** Ash logs, Iron ore, beast hide, riverfish oils, crude relic dust

**Enemies:** Wolves, bandits, marsh crawlers, low shamblers

**Example items:**
- Frontier Blade
- Ash Recurve
- Emberwood Staff
- Scout Leathers
- Ironbound Vest

**Special effects:** Simple, small bonuses, starter utility

---

## Tier 2: Hardened

**Purpose:** Players start defining builds

**Materials:** Oak or Willow, Steel alloy, alpha fangs, shrine ash, rare hide

**Enemies:** Alpha beasts, ruin guards, grave hounds, outlaw captains

**Example items:**
- Fangsplitter Axe
- Hunter's Longbow
- Graveglass Wand
- Hardened Brigandine
- Bastion Kite Shield

**Special effects:** Crit bonuses, bleed chance, trap damage, mana efficiency, stagger resistance

---

## Tier 3: Runic

**Purpose:** Serious midgame, dungeon entry tier, recognizable prestige

**Materials:** Ironbark, Silversteel, Rune Ore, warded bone, warden seals

**Enemies:** Dungeon elites, corrupted knights, arcane abominations, shrine wardens

**Example items:**
- Runesever Greatblade
- Stormbinder Bow
- Frostlit Focus
- Wardenplate Harness
- Relicforged Gauntlets

**Special effects:** Elemental scaling, bonus damage to marked targets, prayer interaction, dodge-triggered effects, gear set bonuses start here

---

## Tier 4: Mythic

**Purpose:** Late-game dungeon and boss tier

**Materials:** Ghostwood, Star Metal, sanctified hide, heart-cores, boss carapace, storm organs

**Enemies:** Zone bosses, mythic hunt targets, ascended shrine keepers, volcanic giants

**Example items:**
- Heartforge Hammer
- Skypiercer Bow
- Soulflame Rod
- Titanbone Cuirass
- Shadecoil Blades

**Special effects:** Class-defining bonuses, phase damage windows, resource refund on mastery play, team synergy effects

---

## Tier 5: Ascendant

**Purpose:** True endgame, rare prestige gear, player aspiration tier

**Materials:** Worldroot fiber, Void Shard, celestial alloy, world-boss trophies, relic hearts, divine catalysts

**Enemies:** World bosses, deep anomaly entities, final raid guardians, eclipse-tier monsters

**Example items:**
- Crownbreaker
- Eclipse Stringbow
- Astral Ruinstaff
- Ashen Sovereign Plate
- Veilfang Set

**Special effects:** Visible aura, transformative passives, playstyle-defining mechanics, special finishing animations, partial boss mechanic resistance

---

## Crafting Formula

Best gear should usually require:

1. 1 base crafted frame
2. 1–3 elite monster parts
3. 1 boss trophy
4. 1 sanctified or attuned reagent
5. Stat requirements to equip

That is the progression spine.

### Example Weapon Path: Skypiercer Bow

**Requirements:**
- Ranged scaling class
- Woodcutting 70
- Smithing 62
- Agility 55
- Boss kill: Storm Matriarch

**Materials:**
- 1 Storm Yew Core
- 2 Tempest Tendons
- 6 Silversteel Bindings
- 1 Matriarch Crest
- 1 Blessed String

This is good because it forces gathering, crafting, PvM, and progression.

---

## Endgame Equipment Philosophy

The player should not just loot final gear directly. They should often loot the key component to finish it.

**Example:**
- Boss drops Heart of the Colossus
- You still need Star Metal plating
- You still need Prayer sanctification
- You still need Smithing level
- You still need the forge unlocked

That makes boss drops exciting without making all other systems meaningless.

---

## Boss Reward Table Philosophy

Each boss should give:

- One guaranteed progression material
- One random class-specific item chance
- One rare enhancement component
- One cosmetic chance
- One low-chance jackpot item

That keeps every kill relevant.
